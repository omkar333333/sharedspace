How It Works

Borrowing and sharing equipment has never been easier.

1. Find What You Need

Browse our catalog of equipment, electronics, and tools listed by other students. Use our smart recommendations if your preferred item is busy.

2. Book & Waitlist

Request a booking for the time you need. If the item is currently in use, you can join the waitlist and get notified automatically when it's free.

3. Scan & Use

When you pick up the equipment, simply scan its QR code to check in. Scan it again when returning it to securely check out and report its condition.
